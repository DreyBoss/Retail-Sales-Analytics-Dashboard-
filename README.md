# Retail-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard-
# Comprehensive Retail Sales & Operations Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end performance analysis of a retail business using an interactive Microsoft Excel workbook. The analysis evaluates total revenue streams, category trends, regional customer acquisition, order fulfillment logistics, and individual customer buying behaviors to identify actionable growth strategies.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary (KPI Snapshot)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $2.26M
* **Total Orders Processed:** 4,922
* **Unique Products Sold:** 17
* **Total Customer Base:** 793
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $459.42
* **Customer Repeat Rate:** 98.36%
* **Average Shipping Duration:** 3.96 Days

---

## 🔍 Detailed Analytical Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
* **Category Drivers:** Technology generated the highest sales revenue at **$827.46k**, followed by Furniture (**$728.38k**) and Office Supplies (**$705.42k**).
* **Top Selling Products:** Phones led overall product sales at **$327.78k**, followed closely by Chairs (**$322.54k**) and Storage (**$219.34k**).
* **Seasonality Trend:** Sales steadily rise throughout the year, peaking in **November ($350.16k)** due to Q4 holiday demand.
* **Fulfillment Preference:** Standard Class is the predominant shipping mode, generating **$1.34M** in total revenue across **2,945 orders**.

### 2. Customer Segmentation & Behavior
* **Dominant Segment:** The **Consumer** category represents **51%** of total sales (**$1.15M**) and largest customer count (**409 customers**).
* **High Customer Loyalty:** Demonstrates a **98.36% repeat purchase rate**, with 337 customers placing 7+ orders and 354 placing 4–6 orders.
* **Top Revenue Contributor:** **Sean Miller** generated the highest individual customer sales at **$25.04k**.

### 3. Regional & Geographical Footprint
* **Top Region:** The **West Region** leads in both revenue (**$710.22k**) and total customers (**255**).
* **Top State Performance:** **California** represents the largest single-state market at **$446.31k**, followed by New York (**$306.36k**) and Texas (**$168.57k**).
* **Top Performing City:** **New York City** leads all metro areas with **$252.46k** in overall sales.

### 4. Supply Chain & Logistics Operations
* **Order Peak Volume:** Order fulfillment reaches its monthly maximum in **November (743 orders)**.
* **Fulfillment SLA:** Most orders (**28%**) take **4 days** to deliver, with an overall average shipping duration of **3.96 days**.
* **Delivery Speed by Mode:** Same Day delivery averages **0 days**, First Class averages **2 days**, Second Class averages **3 days**, and Standard Class averages **5 days**.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Capitalize on Q4 Seasonality:** Allocate additional marketing spend and inventory buffer stock starting in September/October to handle the November sales spike (743 orders).
2. **Standard Class Logistics Optimization:** Standard Class drives the highest order volume (2,945 orders) with an average 5-day delivery turnaround; optimizing warehouse picking times could improve customer satisfaction.
3. **Targeted Regional Expansion:** California and New York generate disproportionately high sales; targeting similar urban demographics in lower-volume states (such as the South region) offers high-margin growth potential.
